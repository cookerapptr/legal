Profilin: görünen ad, kullanıcı adı, kısa biyografi, avatarın ve rengi, hesabının gizli olup olmadığı, takipçi ve gönderi sayıların, arkadaş kodun.

Keşfet’te paylaştığın gönderiler: yemek fotoğrafı ve istersen eklediğin selfie, yazı, kategori, kendi verdiğin puan, bağlı tarif, paylaştığın sofra menüsü ve varsa birlikte pişirdiğin kişi. Yorumların, beğenilerin ve kaydettiklerin.

Kendi yazdığın bir tarifi kaydettiğinde tarifin adı ve fotoğrafı (eklediysen) Keşfet’te gönderi olarak paylaşılır; malzemeleri ve adımları telefonunda kalır.

Kimi takip ettiğin, kimin seni takip ettiği ve bekleyen takip istekleri.

Engellediğin kişiler ve gönderdiğin şikayetler.

Davetler, arkadaşlık istekleri ve iki telefonla oynanan oyunların mesajları için kısa ömürlü bir posta kutusu: kayıt karşı tarafa ulaşınca silinir, ulaşmazsa yedi gün sonra otomatik silinir.

Fotoğraflar ve kamera

Galeriden fotoğraf seçerken iOS’un kendi seçicisi açılır ve uygulamaya yalnızca seçtiğin kareyi verir; galerinin geri kalanını göremeyiz, bunun için izin de istemeyiz. Kamerayla çekmek istersen kamera izni isteriz; çekilen kare yalnızca paylaşmak istediğin gönderi için kullanılır.

Paylaştığın fotoğraf küçültülüp sunucumuzdaki fotoğraf deposuna yüklenir. Depo, fotoğrafın tam adresini bilen herkese açıktır; adresler tahmin edilemez ve yalnızca gönderiyi görebilenlere ulaşır.

Fotoğrafın sunucuya yüklendiği anda konum bilgisi (EXIF) uygulama tarafından kullanılmaz.

Kimler neyi görür

Profil kartın (ad, kullanıcı adı, biyografi, avatar, sayılar) giriş yapmış herkese açıktır. Hesabın gizliyse gönderilerini yalnızca onayladığın takipçiler görür; açıksa giriş yapmış herkes görür.

Takipçi ve takip listelerin giriş yapmış herkese açıktır.

Arkadaş kodun (CK-…) yalnızca onu paylaştığın kişilerde ve karşılıklı takipleştiğin kişilerde görünür (arkadaş listesi yeni bir telefonda bununla kurulur); başkaları onu listeleyemez. Kodunu bilen herkes sana arkadaşlık isteği ve pişirme daveti gönderebilir.

Engellediğin kişi gönderilerini göremez, sana mesaj yazamaz, seni takip edemez ve sana davet gönderemez. Engellendiği ona söylenmez.

İki telefonla pişirirken ve oynarken

Birlikte pişirirken ve iki telefonla oynarken (Ne Yesek?, Eleme Turnuvası, Veto, Buzdolabı) adım işaretlemeleri, sayaçlar, oylar ve seçimler sunucumuz üzerinden anlık olarak karşı telefona iletilir; bunlar saklanmaz.

Karşı telefon o an kapalıysa davet, oy, liste ve “çıktım” gibi mesajlar yukarıdaki posta kutusunda bekler; karşı tarafa ulaşınca silinir.

Telefonunda kalanlar

Favorilerin, pişirme geçmişin ve rozetlerin, kendi yazdığın tariflerin malzemeleri ve adımları, yarım kalan tarifin, tariflere verdiğin puan ve notlar, arkadaş listen, sohbetlerinin telefondaki kopyası ve okunma zamanları, bildirimlerin, şartları kabul ettiğin an, dil ve tema tercihin yalnızca telefonunda saklanır. Bunlar bize gönderilmez.

Hesabından çıkınca bunlar telefonunda kalır ve aynı hesapla tekrar girince yerine gelir. Telefona başka bir hesapla girilirse arkadaş listen, sohbetlerin, bildirimlerin, yarım kalan tarifin ve puanların silinir; favorilerin, pişirme geçmişin, rozetlerin ve kendi tariflerin ise telefonda kalır ve o hesap da onları görür. Hesabını silersen bunların hepsi telefonundan da silinir; yalnızca cihazın ayarları (dil, tema, ses), reklam sayaçları ve reklamla açtığın tariflerin süresi kalır. Uygulamayı silersen hepsi gider. Yedekleri yoktur.

Privacy Policy

2026-10-04

Cooker is an app for couples who cook together. This page explains what information is handled when you use it, where it lives and who can see it. Nothing has been left out for the sake of brevity.

Data controller

The controller of your personal data in Cooker (the “data controller” under Turkish law no. 6698, KVKK, and the EU GDPR) is the app’s developer: the person shown as Cooker’s seller on the App Store. Cooker is made by an individual developer, not a company.

For anything about this policy or your data, write to: hello.cooker.app@gmail.com

An account is required

Cooker cannot be used without an account: cooking invitations, friendships and Discover posts all need to know who you are.

You can sign in with email and password, with Google or with Apple. Your credential record is held by Google’s Firebase Authentication service; we never see your password.

If you sign in with Google, your Google account’s email address, name and profile photo address go into that credential record; your name is offered as a suggestion when you set up your profile. If you sign in with Apple, we receive your email address and (only on the first sign-in) your name; the name is used only as a suggestion while you set up your profile, on this phone. Your contacts, photos and anything else are never accessed.

When signing in with Apple you can hide your email; if you do, we only see the relay address Apple provides.

What is stored on our server

The following data is stored on our server, hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt). The social side of the app cannot work without it:

Your profile: display name, username, short bio, avatar and colour, whether your account is private, your follower and post counts, and your friend code.

The posts you share in Discover: the dish photo and, if you add one, a selfie, caption, category, your own rating, the linked recipe, a table menu you share and, if you cooked together, your partner. Your comments, likes and saved posts.

When you save a recipe you wrote, its name and photo (if you added one) are shared as a post in Discover; its ingredients and steps stay on your phone.

Who you follow, who follows you, and pending follow requests.

Messages you exchange with friends, and the recipe and post links you send them. Messages are NOT end-to-end encrypted; they are stored in plain text on the server, where we could technically read them. We only do so when a legal obligation or a reported abuse case requires it.

The people you block and the reports you send.

A short-lived mailbox for invitations, friend requests and the messages of games played on two phones: an entry is deleted as soon as it reaches the other person, or automatically after seven days if it never does.

Photos and camera

When you pick a photo from your library, iOS’s own picker opens and hands the app only the photo you choose; we cannot see the rest of your library and do not ask for permission to. If you want to take a photo, we ask for camera access; the shot is used only for the post you want to share.

A photo you share is resized and uploaded to the photo store on our server. The store is reachable by anyone who knows a photo’s exact address; addresses cannot be guessed and only reach people who can see the post.

Location data embedded in photos (EXIF) is not used by the app.

Who sees what

Your profile card (name, username, bio, avatar, counts) is visible to every signed-in user. If your account is private, only followers you approve see your posts; otherwise every signed-in user does.

Your follower and following lists are visible to every signed-in user.

Your friend code (CK-…) is known only to the people you share it with and to people you follow mutually (your friend list is rebuilt from that on a new phone); it cannot be listed by others. Anyone who has your code can send you a friend request or a cooking invitation.

A person you block cannot see your posts, message you, follow you or invite you. They are not told they were blocked.

While cooking and playing on two phones

When you cook together or play on two phones (What to Eat?, Knockout Tournament, Veto, Fridge), step check-marks, timers, votes and picks are relayed to the other phone in real time through our server; they are not stored.

If the other phone is off at that moment, messages such as an invitation, a vote, a list or “I left” wait in the mailbox above and are deleted once they arrive.

What stays on your phone

Your favourites, cooking history and badges, the ingredients and steps of the recipes you write, your unfinished recipe draft, the ratings and notes you give recipes, your friend list, the phone’s copy of your chats and when you read them, notifications, when you accepted the terms, and your language and theme preference are stored only on your phone. They are not sent to us.

When you sign out, this data stays on your phone and comes back when you sign in again with the same account. If someone signs in with a different account on this phone, your friend list, chats, notifications, recipe draft and ratings are removed; your favourites, cooking history, badges and own recipes stay on the phone and that account sees them too. Keep this in mind if you share your phone.

If you delete your account, all of this is removed from your phone too; only the device settings (language, theme, sound), the ad counters and the unlock times of recipes you opened with an ad remain. If you delete the app, everything goes. There is no backup.

Ads

Gourmet recipes are locked: watch a short ad and that recipe stays open for 24 hours. Apart from that, a full-screen ad may appear every few recipes.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may process your IP address, technical details of your device, your approximate location and (if you allow it) your advertising identifier.

On iOS you are asked separately for permission to show personalised ads. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

Recipe images

The images of the recipes in the app ship with the app; opening a recipe sends no request over the internet for its image. Photos shared by you and others load from our server.

Why we process data, and on what legal basis

Creating your account and delivering your profile, posts, messages and invitations to you and your friends: necessary to provide the service in the terms of use (KVKK art. 5/2-c, GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).

Reviewing reports, preventing abuse and spam, keeping the app secure, and keeping the app free with non-personalised ads: our legitimate interest (KVKK art. 5/2-f, GDPR art. 6(1)(f)).

Keeping data or handing it to an authority when the law requires it: legal obligation (KVKK art. 5/2-ç, GDPR art. 6(1)(c)).

Personalised ads: only if you grant the permission iOS asks for, i.e. with your explicit consent (KVKK art. 5/1, GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Who we work with

Service providers that process your data: Google (Firebase Authentication for sign-in and Google sign-in; AdMob for ads), Supabase (database, photo store and real-time messaging; Frankfurt), Apple (Sign in with Apple). Each receives only what its job requires.

We do not sell your data. We run no analytics tool that tracks your behaviour in the app. We do not track your location.

Transfers abroad

Your data is processed on servers outside Turkey: Supabase’s servers are in Germany (EU, Frankfurt); the services of Google (Firebase Authentication, Google sign-in, AdMob) and Apple (Sign in with Apple) run in the United States and in these companies’ data centres in other countries.

The transfer is needed to provide your account and the app’s social features; the data transferred is limited to what this policy describes.

How long it is kept

Data on the server is kept until you delete your account. When you delete it via Settings → Delete account, your profile, posts, comments, likes, saved posts, follows, messages (on both sides of a chat), blocks, reports and photo folder are removed from the server and your Firebase credential record is closed.

Mailbox entries are deleted when they reach the other person, or after seven days if they never do.

When you delete a post, its photos are deleted from the store too; if the connection drops at that moment, a photo may stay in your folder until you delete your account. If your partner deletes a joint post, its photos stay in the folder of whoever uploaded them until that person deletes their account; since the post is gone, they no longer appear in the app.

A comment you leave on someone else’s post is deleted with that post or when you delete your account. A joint post you shared together stays until either of you deletes it or the person who posted it deletes their account; if your partner deletes their account, the post stays and their name is removed from it.

Server backups are rotated within 30 days at most; deleted data does not remain in backups beyond that.

Children

Cooker is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe your child has sent us data, write to the address below and we will remove it.

Your rights

Article 11 of the KVKK and the EU GDPR give you the right to find out whether and what data is processed, why it is processed and to whom it is transferred, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to its processing, and to claim compensation if unlawful processing harmed you.

You can edit your profile in the app and delete your account directly via Settings → Delete account. For anything else, write to the address below and we will answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Kurulu) or, in the EU, to your country’s data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we update the date on this page. If there is a meaningful change to what is collected, we will also say so in the app.
